About Us

A genuine family-run garage at the heart of Campbell Road, Brighton.

Welcome to Argyle Tyres & Auto Service — a real family-run garage on Campbell Road in the heart of Brighton. We've been keeping local cars on the road for years, and it's the kind of place where you get greeted by name, the kettle is always on, and you always get a straight answer instead of being sold work you don't need.

Dimitri started it all over 40 years ago. He trained as a mechanic at a time when you learned your trade properly — by hand, under the bonnet, one car at a time — and he's still doing what he loves most: getting stuck in and making sure every single vehicle leaves safe and sound. His health is still as good as his eyesight for spotting a worn ball joint, and his word is his bond.

Alongside him is Peter, family through and through, who looks after the day-to-day running of the workshop. He deals with the suppliers, chases down parts, sorts the paperwork and, most importantly, keeps the place friendly and welcoming for everyone who walks through the door. Between the two of them, you'll always find one of the family at work.

Because we're family run, your car is never just a number on a job sheet. We'll tell you honestly what needs doing now, what can wait, and what simply isn't worth spending money on. That's the way we'd want to be treated ourselves — and it's the way we've always treated our customers.

— The family at Argyle

Our Services

Everything your car needs, under one roof — full standard auto care carried out by experienced technicians.

Tyres & Alignment

Budget to premium brands, fitted on site with balancing and wheel alignment for a smooth, safe ride.

Servicing & MOT

Full and interim servicing with genuine parts, plus MOT testing and preparation done right.

Brakes & Clutch

Pads, discs, callipers, cylinders and clutch work — inspected, diagnosed and repaired to keep you stopping safely.

Diagnostics & Repairs

Modern computer diagnostics to find faults fast, from engine lights to electrical gremlins.

Exhausts & Batteries
